Source code for my personal portfolio: a site built around the two sides of how I work, **cybersecurity and art**.

---

## The Concept

Most security portfolios show only the technical side. This one is built on a **cyber / art duality**: the analytical work of investigating systems next to the creative work (painting, drawing, photography, woodworking) that shapes how I approach problems. Patience, precision, and seeing patterns show up in both.

The design carries that idea through, from the split cyber/art portrait on the landing page to separate sections for each side.

---

## What's on the Site

| Page | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| **Cyber** | CTF investigations, DFIR and log analysis, home lab engineering, and my SOC automation projects |
| **Art** | Paintings, drawings, and photography, with the story behind each piece |
| **About** | Background, U.S. Navy service, Virginia Tech coursework, and a certifications timeline |
| **Contact** | How to reach me |

Security write-ups focus on **methodology, evidence, and conclusions**. Some lab and CTF write-ups are summarized rather than published step by step, to respect the rules of the platforms they came from.
